About Me

Senior AI Engineer & Full Stack Developer · Istanbul, Türkiye

I'm a Senior AI Engineer & Full Stack Developer with 15+ years in software and 2+ years shipping LLM systems. I work forward-deployed: I sit with operations, support and sales teams, map how the work really flows — data, decisions, risks — and turn it into AI that runs in production, not in a demo.

On the AI side I build tool-calling agents with structured output, ground them with PageIndex-style RAG, wrap them in guardrails (read-only query sandboxes, data masking, code-level safety checks) and run them on multiple providers with failover — Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, NVIDIA NIM and self-hosted llama.cpp — with usage limits, token tracking and cost logs. I also design the conversation, persona and decision rules the agents follow, and expose data to them through MCP servers.

Backend is polyglot: Python for automation and integration services, Go for real-time and LLM services, PHP/Laravel for product platforms and Node.js for real-time and bot backends. I ship edge-native products with Next.js on Cloudflare Workers (D1, R2, KV) and run everything on Docker, Kubernetes and GitHub Actions, with an autonomous AI review agent in the loop.
